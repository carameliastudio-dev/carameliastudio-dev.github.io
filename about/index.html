PDF editor & scanner for Android

Sign PDFs, fill forms, merge, split and compress files in a few taps. Everything happens on your phone: no account, no uploads.

  • No watermarkYour files stay clean, ever.
  • No subscriptionEvery tool works. Remove ads once if you like.
  • No uploadsYour files never leave your phone.

Become a beta tester

Free to use with ads. A one-time purchase removes ads forever. Signatures are visual signatures, not certificate-based digital signatures.

What it can do

  • Sign & fill forms

    Draw, type or scan your signature. Fill text, checkboxes and dates.

  • Scan to PDF

    Camera scans with automatic edge detection and cleanup.

  • Merge, split & organize

    Combine files, split pages, reorder and rotate.

  • Compress & convert

    Fit under upload limits. PDF to images and back.

  • Password protect

    Lock a PDF, or change a password you know.

  • Watermark & numbers

    Add watermarks, page numbers, headers and footers.
